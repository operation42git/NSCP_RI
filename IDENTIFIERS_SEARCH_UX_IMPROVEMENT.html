Current vs. Proposed UX

Current Issues

  • Limited information in search results
  • No dangerous goods indicator visible
  • No transport/equipment details
  • Requires extra click to see details
  • Users can't make informed decisions

Proposed Solution

  • All key information in search results
  • Dangerous goods flag prominently displayed
  • Transport & equipment summaries visible
  • Expandable details for full information
  • One-click access to complete details

Key UX Improvements

Visibility
All critical information (dangerous goods, transport mode, equipment counts) visible in summary view
Reduced Clicks
Users can make decisions without clicking through to detail page. Expandable cards provide full details when needed.
Dangerous Goods Flag
Prominently displayed with color coding (red=yes, green=no, gray=N/A) for quick scanning
Equipment Hierarchy
Clear visual distinction between used equipment and carried equipment with nested display
Progressive Disclosure
Summary view shows essentials, expandable details show full information without overwhelming the list
Responsive Design
Adapts to different screen sizes with flexible grid layout and mobile-friendly card design
